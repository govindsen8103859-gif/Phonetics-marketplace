Phonetics — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Phonetics is a prototype marketplace concept for used mobile phones. This version is a front-end demo and does not send form submissions or photos to a server.

Information submitted

Any information typed into the demo forms stays in the browser unless you separately share it. Do not enter real phone numbers, addresses, identity documents, or other sensitive information in this demo.

Photos and device data

Photo selection is only a local browser preview/selection in this prototype. Files are not uploaded or stored by this site.

Future version

Before accepting real listings, Phonetics must add a secure backend, administrator authentication, protected storage, and a final privacy policy describing actual data processing and retention.

Contact

No support contact has been configured yet.
